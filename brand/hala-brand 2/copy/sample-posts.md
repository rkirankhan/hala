# Hala — sample social post copy (EN + DE)

Written in the site's voice: plain-spoken, concrete, second person, no AI jargon.
German follows the site's register — **Sie** throughout, Hala named as the subject,
24-hour times, prices in euros.

**One rule that overrides everything:** never claim an integration that isn't
confirmed, never claim the agent switches language per caller (one agent, one
language), and any AI must be disclosed as AI — that includes replies from the
Hala account itself.

## Caption conventions

- **CTA is always the demo**, phrased as the site phrases it: "we ring your number with Hala live and you throw whatever you like at it."
- **Numbers do the talking**: first ring, 60 seconds, 24 hours, 48 hours to live, 24/7. Never vague superlatives ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
- **Scenes over claims**: start with a time and a situation (Friday 19:42, plating desserts) rather than a feature name.
- **Hashtags**: 3–5 on Instagram, 0–3 on LinkedIn, none needed on Facebook. Keep them concrete (#Restaurants, #SalonLife), never AI-hype tags.
- **No customer numbers or testimonials yet** — the proof section is reserved until a named customer agrees to be quoted. Don't invent stats.
- **Disclose the AI.** If Hala itself ever replies on social channels, it says it's an AI — same rule as the agent's opening line.
